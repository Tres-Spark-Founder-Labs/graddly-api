10. Release Phases

10.1 Phase 1 — MVP Launch (Q3 2026)

- The MVP must include all Must Have requirements across all four portals.

- The Levy Exchange (FlowPortal Module A) launches in assisted mode in Phase 1 — matching is facilitated by the Gradlly team using platform data, not automated.

- Full automated matching launches in Phase 2.

**Phase:** Phase 1 MVP scope per portal

Portal 1 (Employer): F1.1.1, F1.1.2, F1.1.3, F1.2.1, F1.2.2, F1.2.3, F1.2.4, F1.2.5, F1.3.1, F1.3.2, F1.3.3

Portal 2 (Provider): F2.1.1, F2.1.2, F2.1.4, F2.2.1, F2.2.2, F2.2.3, F2.2.4, F2.3.1, F2.3.2, F2.4.1

Portal 3 (Apprentice): F3.1.1, F3.1.2, F3.1.3, F3.1.4, F3.2.1, F3.2.2, F3.2.3, F3.2.4, F3.3.1, F3.3.2, F3.3.4, F3.4.1, F3.4.2, F3.4.3

Portal 4 (FlowPortal): F4.1.1, F4.1.2, F4.2.1, F4.2.2, F4.2.3, F4.2.4, F4.3.1, F4.3.2, F4.3.4, F4.4.1, F4.4.2, F4.4.3

10.2 Phase 2 — Post-Launch (Months 7–18)

**Phase:** Phase 2 delivers all Should Have requirements and key Could Have enhancements. The Levy Exchange automated matching engine is the primary Phase 2 engineering investment.

**Phase:** Phase 2 priority features

Portal 1: F1.1.4 (Levy Transfer Hub full automation), F1.1.5 (report scheduling), F1.4.1 (ROI report), F1.4.2 (provider performance comparison)

Portal 2: F2.1.3 (SAR auto-generation), F2.2.5 (tutor caseload management), F2.4.2 (employer visit log), F2.4.3 (satisfaction survey)

Portal 3: F3.3.3 (reflective statement tool), native iOS and Android mobile app

Portal 4: F4.1.3 (transfer preferences), F4.1.4 (donor analytics portal), F4.3.3 (funding claim tracker), F4.4.4 (applied project submission), F4.4.5 (async learning modules)

Platform: ML-based donor-SME matching engine, SMS notifications (P3), enterprise API for levy donors

10.4 Won't Have (Now)

The following items are explicitly out of scope for Phases 1, 2, and 3 and are acknowledged for potential future consideration only:

AI-powered essay or portfolio marking (requires EPAO approval — not within Gradlly's regulatory scope)

Video-based OTJ evidence capture within the mobile app (storage and bandwidth implications deferred)

Integrated payroll or HR system (Gradlly is a learning management platform, not an HRIS)

Consumer-facing apprenticeship job board (B2C marketplace is a separate business model)

## Ofsted inspection scheduling or direct communication with HMI inspectors
